About

I am a researcher and educator specialising in sustainable urban mobility and transport systems, with over 15 years of academic and research experience across Ukraine, the United Kingdom and Europe.

My research focuses on micromobility, public transport integration, urban logistics and sustainable transport policies. I combine quantitative and qualitative research methods to investigate transport systems, mobility behaviour and policy challenges.

I am currently a Senior Research Fellow at the University of Rome Tor Vergata, Italy, where my research investigates the potential of micromobility integration with public transport in the context of Ukraine's post-war recovery.

My professional activities include international research collaboration, European-funded projects, academic teaching and knowledge transfer between research institutions, policymakers and transport professionals.
